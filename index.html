My Story

I’m Varun Patel, a curious learner who had always dreamed of building my own apps. For a long time, that dream felt out of reach because I had never written a line of code.

Exploring ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google Gemini helped me see a different starting point. AI tools offered a way to ask questions, explore ideas, and make unfamiliar code less intimidating. What inspired me was not the possibility of skipping the learning—it was the possibility of finally beginning.

That curiosity sparked the question that inspired my first step: What if I could learn as I build?

With a new MacBook Air and a willingness to start from scratch, I turned that question into action. I wanted to create iOS apps that felt simple, useful, and intuitive—turning ideas into something people could actually use. That motivation gave my learning a purpose, and the journey led to EasyList and TripMate.

Today, I share a happy life with my beautiful wife, Smruti, and our two children—our handsome son, Jenil, and our little princess, Anaya. Alongside family life and my career, I’m making room for a dream that once felt distant.

If there is something you have been waiting to try, I hope my story encourages you to give it a chance. You can be a beginner and still have something worthwhile to create. Start with a question, take one small step, and give yourself permission to learn along the way.
