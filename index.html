My Story

I’m Varun Patel, a curious learner who had always dreamed of building my own apps.

Exploring AI tools helped me see a different starting point: ask questions, explore ideas, and make unfamiliar code less intimidating.

That curiosity sparked the question: What if I could learn as I build?

With a new MacBook Air and a willingness to start from scratch, I began turning ideas into useful iOS apps.

Today, I share a happy life with my beautiful wife, Smruti, and our two children—Jenil and Anaya.
