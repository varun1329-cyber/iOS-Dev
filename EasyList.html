Core Features

Smart Item Entry

Type and press return to add items. The app smartly auto-capitalizes, sorts alphabetically, and prevents duplicates.

Use Your Voice [Hands-Free]

Use the microphone to add items hands-free using integrated Apple speech recognition.

Live Progress Tracking

Tap any item to toggle its completed status, and watch the progress bar update in real time.

Easy Clean Up

Clear completed items or reset the entire checklist instantly with one-tap confirmation actions.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our app.

EasyList collects and stores only basic shopping list items locally on your device.

This includes:

  • Item names
  • Item creation timestamp
  • Item completion status
  • Total Count of items
  • Total Count of completed items

No personal information, contacts, location data, or usage tracking is collected or shared.

All of the above item data are saved locally on your device in a JSON file within the app's documents directory. Your data is not transmitted to any external servers or third parties.

You have full control over your data: you can add or delete items at any time, and the app updates the stored data accordingly.
