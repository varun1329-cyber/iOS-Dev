Core Features

Unlimited Lists

Create as many lists as you like: groceries, errands, tasks, travel, books to read. The home screen shows every list, newest first, with a status chip (Empty, Active, or Completed) and a completed/total count. Quick-start names make new lists effortless, and a swipe deletes one with confirmation.

Smart Item Entry

Type and press Done to add items. Text is title-cased as you type (olive oil becomes Olive Oil), items are listed alphabetically and numbered, and duplicate protection with simple plural handling keeps your list clean.

Continuous Voice Input [Hands-Free]

Tap the mic and speak items back to back, with no commas or pauses (“bread butter apples chips”). A floating Listening… pill with a Done button stays on screen while the mic is live, powered by integrated Apple speech recognition.

Review Before You Add

A review sheet lets you edit names, uncheck or delete items, or Toggle All before adding. Duplicates are crossed out, labelled, and never added, even as you edit. Voice-added items get a small blue microphone badge.

Private iCloud Sync

Lists sync across your own devices automatically through your private iCloud account (CloudKit). The developer cannot read them, and the app works fully offline when iCloud is unavailable.

Collaborate & Share

Share a list with family or housemates through an invite-only iCloud link sent via Messages, WhatsApp, Mail, or AirDrop. Collaborators can read and edit, and every completed item shows “Done by” with the name of who finished it. You can also send a plain-text copy through the iOS share sheet.

Live Progress Tracking

Tap a row to mark it complete: it turns green, is struck through, and shows a timestamp. Each list has its own progress bar that updates in real time, and toasts confirm every action.

Easy Clean Up

Use the list menu to Clear All Completed or Clear Everything, or delete any single item with the × icon. Onboarding, a Helpful Tips guide, and Settings keep everything easy to learn and manage.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important. This policy summarizes how VocaList handles your information.

VocaList has no developer servers, accounts, analytics, ads, tracking, or third-party SDKs. The developer does not receive your data.

Information Stored by the App

The app stores the following:

  1. List names and creation dates
  2. Item names and quantities
  3. Item completion status and timestamps
  4. The display name of whoever last completed an item
  5. Total and completed item counts

Storage and iCloud

Your lists are saved on your device in a JSON file within the app’s documents directory and in your own private iCloud account (CloudKit) so they sync across your devices. The developer cannot read them. If iCloud is unavailable, the app works locally.

Voice

While the microphone is on, audio is passed to Apple’s Speech Recognition, which may process it on-device or on Apple’s servers depending on your device and language. VocaList never saves audio, and only the items you confirm on the review sheet are stored.

Sharing

Shared lists, including display names on items, are visible to the people you invite. Your display name defaults to your device name, so change it during onboarding if it contains your real name and you plan to share lists.

Ratings and Feedback

Ratings and comments you enter stay on your device and are never uploaded.

Your Control Over Your Data

You have full control over your data: add or delete items and lists at any time, or use Clear All Data in Settings to remove lists from the device. Lists still in iCloud sync back until deleted individually.
